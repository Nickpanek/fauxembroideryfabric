## Design Story

This pattern captures the timeless elegance of hand-embroidered botanical sketches, inspired by 18th-century French textile traditions. Each flower and leaf is carefully rendered with delicate line work that mimics the appearance of traditional blue work embroidery.

## Care Instructions

Machine wash cold, gentle cycle. Tumble dry low or line dry. Iron on medium heat if needed. The faux embroidery print maintains its crisp appearance wash after wash.
