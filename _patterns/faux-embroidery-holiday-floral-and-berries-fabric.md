This faux embroidery fabric blends gold thread tones, evergreen leaves, and crimson berries to evoke hand-stitched winter florals. The design gives the illusion of real embroidery, ideal for holiday quilting and decor.

**Features**
- Embroidery-style stitched effect without actual threadwork  
- Holiday florals and berries motif  
- Suitable for quilting and home decor  
- Balanced color palette of red, green, and gold  
- Works well as a cheater quilt base
